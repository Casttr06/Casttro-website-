Artist statement

Built from pressure, not performance.

CASTTRO is an independent artist out of Perth creating hip-hop that feels lived-in, not manufactured. The writing moves through identity, ambition, doubt, and growth without losing the weight that makes the music replayable.

The mask is part of the language, but not as a gimmick. It represents reflection, distortion, and the version of yourself you only meet when everything gets quiet. That is why the line stays the same: it’s not a mask, it’s a mirror.

Every track should leave a bruise and a thought.
