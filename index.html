Casttro is an independent hip-hop artist based in Perth, Western Australia, known for blending raw lyricism with moody, melodic production and a powerful live presence.

Driven by storytelling and symbolism, Casttro wears a mask as part of his visual identity - not to conceal, but to reflect. The mask acts as both mirror and canvas, challenging listeners to see themselves in his music.

His music explores growth, self-reflection, and cultural identity through layered lyrics and striking aesthetics. With over 60,000 total streams and 13,000+ monthly listeners, Casttro has built a catalogue that continues to resonate.
