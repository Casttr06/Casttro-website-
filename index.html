CASTTRO

Artist Bio

Casttro | Perth, Western Australia
60K+ Total streams
13K+ Monthly listeners
2023 WAM Awards - Promise placed 3rd

Casttro is an independent hip-hop artist based in Perth, Western Australia, known for blending raw lyricism with moody, melodic production and a powerful live presence.

Driven by storytelling and symbolism, Casttro wears a mask as part of his visual identity - not to conceal, but to reflect. The mask acts as both mirror and canvas, challenging listeners to see themselves in his music and inviting deeper connection through anonymity redefined.

His music explores growth, self-reflection, and cultural identity through layered lyrics and striking aesthetics. With over 60,000 total streams across major platforms and 13,000+ monthly listeners, Casttro has built a catalogue that continues to resonate with audiences and tastemakers.
